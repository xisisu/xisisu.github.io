Research focused on making real-time guarantees work in virtualized environments — work that shipped as [RT-Xen](https://github.com/xen-project/xen/blob/master/xen/common/sched/rt.c) in the Xen hypervisor and influenced cloud scheduling at scale. 10 peer-reviewed publications.
